Staff Recognition

Acknowledge great work the moment it happens — branded, automated, and trackable. No app needed for staff.

  • Branded PDF certificates delivered by email
  • Custom recognition categories per organisation
  • Approval workflow for sensitive categories
  • Nominator confirmation emails
  • Recognition Wall — public staff feed
  • Monthly prize draw with cooldown rules
  • Staff reminder nudge emails
  • Built-in analytics: trends, leaderboards & engagement
  • Branded, board-ready PDF reports

PL Event Booking New

A complete professional learning event management system — from scheduling to certificates — all in one place.

  • Create and manage PL sessions and events
  • Staff self-registration via QR code
  • Live attendance tracking via QR check-in
  • Auto-generated attendance certificates
  • Lunch and catering management
  • CSV export for attendees, sessions, lunch
  • Admin dashboard with event analytics
  • Shareable event links and registration pages

WHS & Incident Reporting New

Log, track and report workplace safety and psychosocial incidents — with a no-login staff reporting form and board-ready records built in from day one.

  • QR code or link staff reporting — no login, anonymous option
  • Physical, psychosocial & near-miss incident types
  • Traffic-light register: open, overdue, closed
  • "Is this notifiable?" helper with state regulator contacts
  • Body-map injury diagrams & photo attachments
  • Tiered access for sensitive psychosocial reports
  • Branded incident PDFs + full register export (5-year retention)
  • Patterns & Insights safety analytics
🗓️

Staff Scheduler New

One live schedule for your whole team — who is where, doing what, every slot of every day. Replaces the roster spreadsheet for good.

  • Master timetable — whole team, one day, one grid
  • Drag-and-drop reassignment, updates everywhere instantly
  • Recurring weekly template + one-off day overrides
  • Unassigned work flagged in red, with weekly coverage summary
  • Auto-save with a full change log — no save button
  • Print-ready PDF exports: master timetable + individual rosters
  • CSV import/export of staff and schedule
  • School Edition available — purpose-built for Education Assistant timetabling and student support allocation
💬

VIBE Pulse — Wellbeing New

Anonymous staff wellbeing check-ins people actually answer — with trends over time and a one-click "what we heard" share-back that closes the loop.

  • Short anonymous pulse — no accounts, single-use links
  • Hard 3-response anonymity floor
  • Wellbeing trends over time
  • Multi-site and group rollups
  • "What we heard" share-back poster and wall post
  • Scheduled weekly, fortnightly or monthly sends
  • Board-ready PDF wellbeing reports
  • Helps meet psychosocial monitoring duties
